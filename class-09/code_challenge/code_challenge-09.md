# 🎯 Code Challenge: Mock Interviews

## 📋 Specifications

* Read **all of the following instructions carefully**.
* Today, you and a peer will take turns interviewing each other with a code challenge.
* The interviewer will score the candidate according to the **[Whiteboard Rubric](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1scthkmARfzAFZSYAp6LA2coOaoWUWbSzMbtIU4jcHw)**.
* Notes can be taken in an electronic copy of the document, or a printout of it.
* In either case, the completed rubric will be given to the candidate for review purposes.
* The candidate submits the rubric with the score they achieved as a candidate.
* Each interview should be **time-boxed to a strict 30 minutes**.

# 📚 Documentation

The interviewer should take **detailed notes** on the Rubric form.

### The interviewer should:

1. Assess points for **every item** on the Rubric.
2. Record the points earned for each item.
3. Add the points together.
4. Record the **total points** on the form.

# 📤 Submission Instructions

After the interview:

### 1. Review Your Rubric

Review the notes your interviewer recorded for you on the Rubric.

### 2. Submit Your Final Result

Submit the final result of your interview **as a candidate**.

Paste the link to the rubric that evaluated you as a candidate:

* A **Google Doc**, or
* A **photo of a printout**

### 3. Add a Reflection

Comment on your submission with a summary of:

* **How it went for you**
* **What went well**
* **What you would like to improve**

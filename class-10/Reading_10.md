# 💡 Ways You Can Teach

Choose a format that helps explain something you learned.

Some ideas for how you might want to teach:

* 💭 **Use an analogy**
* 🔎 **Explain a detail in depth**
* ❓ **Use WHY, WHAT, HOW structure**
* 🚶 **Tutorial / walk through an example**
* 🧠 **Write a quiz**
* 📖 **Create a vocabulary/definition list**
* 📝 **Write a cheat sheet**
* 🖼️ **Create a diagram / visualization / cartoon of a topic**
* 🗣️ **Anthropomorphize the concepts, and write a conversation between them**
* 🗺️ **Build a map of the information**
* ✏️ **Construct a fill-in-the-blank worksheet for the topic**
